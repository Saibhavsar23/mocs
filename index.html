Bottom line

If we run to our projection we finish about 8th in Group 4 and just outside the wild cards. Getting in takes about 12 seconds per runner better than that, a top-5 average near 16:52 on the Holmdel course. That is a good day but not a crazy one: last year the Shore-based projections ran 18 to 21 seconds per runner slower than what runners actually ran at Groups. Treat the odds as a rough guide, not a forecast.

How I got these numbers

  • The rule. The 2026–27 NJSIAA regulations send all six group champions to the MOC automatically, merge every other team, and take the top eight by team score, with ties broken by the sixth runner. The document does not spell out how the merge is scored, so I used the rescoring NJSIAA has used in past years (MileSplit, 2024 wild cards). My rebuild of it reproduced the 2023, 2024 and 2025 MOC fields, and 2022 except for one swap.
  • The field. Group 4 results from the four sectionals on MileSplit. The sectional courses differ, so raw times are not comparable across sections.
  • Holmdel conversion. For each section and group, the median change in per-runner time from the 2025 sectional to the 2025 Groups race among the teams that qualified. Individual teams moved from about 10 to over 100 seconds.

What could change it

  • The conversion comes from one year, with team-to-team scatter of about 20 seconds per runner. Weather on Nov 7 can move everyone together.
  • Non-public schools use their 2025 results, which is a guess. Several of them sit in the wild-card range and could move either way.
  • Groups 1–3 conversions are based on five teams each and are noisier than Group 4's.
  • A ninth or tenth wild card occasionally gets in on head-to-head results. I counted only the top eight.
